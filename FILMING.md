# Filming the pink slip clips (for Balli)

Nine short clips, one per check. A phone is fine. Each clip plays silently on the website, on a loop, next to the check it shows.

## How to film
- Hold the phone sideways (landscape), steady, at about waist height. Lean on something if you can.
- 6 to 10 seconds each. Just do the job normally; don't look at the camera.
- Good light: workshop lights on, roller door open. Avoid filming straight into the doorway light.
- Use a customer's car only with their OK, and keep number plates out of shot (or tell us and we'll blur them).
- No sound needed. Wear the usual work gear.

## The nine clips
1. Tyres: tread depth gauge pressed into the tyre, then read it.
2. Brakes: wheel off, look at the disc and pads with a torch.
3. Steering and suspension: car on the hoist, rock the front wheel, check the joints.
4. Lights and indicators: brake lights and indicators flashing at the back of the car.
5. Windscreen and wipers: lift a wiper, run a thumb along the blade, look at the glass.
6. Seatbelts: pull the driver's belt out fully, buckle and release it.
7. Body and rust: torch along the door sills and wheel arches.
8. Exhaust: under the car on the hoist, check the exhaust and its rubber mounts.
9. LPG: boot open, spray leak detector on the tank fittings (LPG cars only).

## Sending them
Send the original files (WhatsApp is fine, or Google Drive for best quality). AMBS will trim, compress and add them. Until a clip arrives, that check keeps its current picture.
