Pink slip vs blue slip in NSW: which one do you need?

A pink slip is the yearly safety check most cars over five years old need to renew rego. A blue slip is a safety and identity check for a car that isn't currently registered in NSW, such as one from interstate or with rego expired more than three months.

By Shine Auto Repairs, Warwick Farm15 November 20265 min read

Quick answer

Renewing rego on a car more than five years old? You need a pink slip. It's valid for six months and costs no more than $52 for a light vehicle.

Registering a car that isn't on NSW rego right now? You need a blue slip. It's valid for 42 days and costs no more than $82 for a light vehicle.

What a pink slip is

A pink slip is the everyday name for a NSW safety inspection report. The NSW Government says most light vehicles more than five years old need one each year to renew their registration.

The five years are counted from the month and year on the car's compliance plate. For imported vehicles, the count starts from the month and year of manufacture. So a car with a compliance plate dated March 2021 needs its first pink slip at the rego renewal after March 2026.

The inspection is done at an Authorised Inspection Scheme (AIS) station. When the car passes, the station sends the result to Transport for NSW electronically, so there's no paper slip for you to post off. The report is valid for six months, and you need to renew your rego within that time.

You can see what goes into the check in our plain-English pink slip checklist.

When you need a blue slip instead

A blue slip covers safety and also confirms the car's identity. NSW requires one if your vehicle:

  • has had its registration expired for more than three months
  • was brought into NSW from another state, territory or country
  • has no number plate
  • is a written-off vehicle that has been approved for re-registration.

It may also be needed after significant changes, such as a new engine, or to clear some defect notices. A blue slip report is valid for 42 days from the date of inspection, so book it close to when you plan to register the car.

How to tell which one your car needs

Start with your rego renewal notice. Service NSW says the notice states whether or not you need an inspection. If it does, and the car is already on NSW rego, that's a pink slip.

If you've just bought a car from Queensland, imported one, or let the rego lapse for more than three months, you're in blue slip territory. If the rego expired less than three months ago, you can usually renew as normal, with a pink slip if the car is over five years old.

Cars under five years old don't need a pink slip to renew. Some vehicles are exempt too, including some historic, conditionally registered and seasonally registered vehicles and some light trailers. The NSW safety checks page lists them.

What each one costs in NSW

NSW sets the most an inspection station can charge. From 1 July 2026, the maximum fees for a light vehicle, including GST, are:

  • pink slip (safety check): $52
  • blue slip (safety and identity check): $82.

Motorcycles and trailers have their own, lower maximums. NSW reviews these fees each 1 July, so check the NSW fees page if you're reading this after June 2027.

If the car doesn't pass

A failed check isn't the end of the road. If you have the repairs done within 14 days and take the car back to the same station to be reassessed, there's no extra inspection fee. After 14 days, you pay for another inspection.

That's one reason to have the check done somewhere that also does repairs. If a worn tyre, a blown globe or a brake issue turns up, it can be looked at or booked in straight away.

Common questions

Does a car under five years old need a pink slip?

No. Most light vehicles only need a pink slip once they're more than five years old, counted from the month and year on the compliance plate. Your rego renewal notice will tell you if an inspection is needed.

How long is a pink slip valid in NSW?

Six months. You need to renew your registration within that time for the report to count.

How long is a blue slip valid?

A blue slip report is valid for 42 days from the date of inspection.

Do I need a blue slip for a car bought in another state?

Yes. A vehicle brought into NSW from another state or territory needs a blue slip before it can be registered here.

What happens if my car fails its pink slip?

Have the repairs done within 14 days and take it back to the same station, and there's no second inspection fee. After 14 days, another fee applies.
