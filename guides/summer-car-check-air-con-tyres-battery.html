Air con, tyres and batteries: signs your car needs attention before summer

Before summer, check three things: air con that blows less cold than it used to, tyres with shallow or uneven tread, and a battery that's slow to start the car. Heat makes all three worse, and they're cheaper to sort before a 40 degree day in western Sydney.

By Shine Auto Repairs, Warwick Farm15 November 20264 min read

Quick answer

Air con: not as cold, weak airflow, a musty smell or new noises. Tyres: tread close to the wear bars, cracks, bulges or uneven wear. Battery: slow cranking, clicking, dim lights at start-up or a battery light on the dash.

Air con: signs it needs a regas or repair

  • It blows cool but not cold, or takes much longer to cool the car than it used to.
  • Airflow is weak even on high, which can point to a blocked cabin filter.
  • A musty or damp smell when you turn it on.
  • A new rattle, squeal or clunk when the air con switches on.
  • It cools for a while, then blows warm.

What a regas is

Car air con works by pumping refrigerant gas around a sealed system. A regas removes what's left in the system, then refills it with the right amount and type of refrigerant for your car.

The system is sealed, so if it's low on gas, there's usually a leak somewhere, even if it's slow. That's why a good air con job checks for leaks rather than just topping up. If a part like the compressor or a fan has failed, a regas alone won't fix it.

The air con also helps clear a fogged windscreen, so take more care in wet weather until it's fixed.

Tyres: signs they're worn

Hot roads and full summer road trips are hard on tyres. Look for:

  • Tread close to the wear bars. Most tyres have small raised bars across the bottom of the grooves. When the tread is level with them, the tyre is worn out.
  • Uneven wear. One edge worn more than the other usually points to wheel alignment or suspension wear. Check the inside edge too, as it's easy to miss.
  • Cracks, cuts or bulges in the sidewall or tread.
  • A tyre that keeps losing pressure, or a tyre pressure light that keeps coming back.
  • Vibration or pulling to one side at speed.

For the pink slip, the NSW safety check standards fail a tyre with less than 1.5mm of tread on the surfaces that touch the road, or with deep cuts, bulges or exposed cords. Grip in the wet drops well before that point, so don't wait for the limit.

Check your tyre pressures

Set them to the pressures on the tyre placard, often inside the driver's door, and check them when the tyres are cold. Low pressure makes a tyre run hotter and wear faster. Don't forget the spare.

Batteries: signs it's on the way out

Heat is hard on car batteries, and a battery weakened over summer often fails on the first cold morning. Watch for:

  • the engine cranking slowly before it starts
  • a clicking sound and no start
  • headlights or dash lights dimming when you start the car
  • white or green crusting on the battery terminals
  • a swollen or cracked battery case
  • needing a jump start more than once.

If your battery is several years old, have it tested before summer. A test checks how well it holds and delivers charge, so you replace it on your own terms rather than in a car park.

Battery light or flat battery?

A battery light on the dash while you're driving is a different problem. It usually means the battery isn't being charged, often because of the alternator, a drive belt or a wiring fault. The car may stop once the battery runs down, so switch off what you don't need and get somewhere safe. Our warning lights guide has more.

Common questions

How do I know if my car air con needs a regas?

If it blows cool but not cold, takes longer to cool the car, or cools then blows warm, it may be low on gas. As the system is sealed, low gas usually means a leak, so it should be checked as well as refilled.

What tyre tread depth fails a NSW pink slip?

The NSW safety check standards fail a tyre with less than 1.5mm of tread on the surfaces that normally touch the road.

What are the signs a car battery needs replacing?

Slow cranking, clicking with no start, lights dimming at start-up, crusted terminals, a swollen case, or needing more than one jump start.
