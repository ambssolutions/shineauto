What a pink slip looks at

The inspection checks that the car is safe to stay on the road. The sheet below is an example of what gets checked, not a result for your car.

Safety check · light vehicleExample only
  • TyresTread depth, cuts, bulges and damage.
  • BrakesThe car pulls up straight, and the handbrake holds.
  • Steering and suspensionPlay in the steering, worn joints and leaking shocks.
  • LightsHeadlights, brake lights, indicators and reversing lights.
  • Windscreen and wipersDamage in the driver's view, and wipers that clear the glass.
  • SeatbeltsEvery belt latches, retracts and isn't frayed.
  • Body and rustRust or damage that weakens the body.
  • ExhaustLeaks, loose mounts and too much smoke or noise.
  • LPG systemOn LPG cars only: the tank, lines and fittings.

If something fails, we tell you what it was. Have it fixed and checked again here within 14 days and there's no second inspection fee.

A pink slip is the safety check to renew the rego on most light vehicles over five years old. A blue slip is needed to register a vehicle: when the rego expired more than three months ago, the vehicle is from interstate or overseas, it has no plates, or it's a written-off vehicle being re-registered.

A pink slip is valid for six months. A blue slip is valid for 42 days.
