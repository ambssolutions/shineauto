Pink slip or blue slip

Both are NSW inspections for rego. Here's which one you need and the most a station can charge.

Pink slip and blue slip compared
QuestionPink slipBlue slip
What it isA safety checkA safety and identity check
When you need itRenewing rego on most light vehicles over five years old, counted from the compliance plate dateRego expired more than three months ago, a vehicle from interstate or overseas, no plates, or a written-off vehicle being re-registered
Report valid for6 months42 days
