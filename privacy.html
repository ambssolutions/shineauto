How we handle your details

Shine Auto Repairs follows the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988. This notice explains what this website collects and why.

Looking up your car by rego

If you use the rego lookup, your plate number and state are sent to our vehicle data provider to find the make, model and year. We don't store the plate, and no owner details are looked up or shown.

The Ask Shine assistant

The website assistant answers from this website's own content, inside your browser. Your questions aren't sent anywhere unless you choose to leave a message, which then goes to the workshop so we can reply.

Messages you send us

If you send us a message, we receive your name, contact details, the vehicle details you give us and your message. We use them only to reply to you about your car.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your details. This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. If you choose to show the map, Google Maps loads and Google's own privacy policy applies to it.

Seeing or correcting your details

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Call us on (02) 8798 5715 or visit the workshop at Unit 4B, 4 Homepride Avenue, Warwick Farm NSW 2170.
